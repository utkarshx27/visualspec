# social-linkedin

## Formats & Dimensions
- `feed_portrait`: 1080x1350 (4:5) — Optimal for feed engagement.
- `square`: 1080x1080 (1:1).
- `banner`: 1200x628 (1.91:1).

## Safe Margins
- 80px all sides for portraits to ensure no clipping on desktop or mobile feeds.

## Design Guidance
- Professional authority: Deep dark backgrounds (#09090B), indigo/cyan/slate accents, crisp clean sans-serif typography.
- Avoid consumer clickbait aesthetics.
- Ideal templates: `product-launch`, `stat-card`, `quote-card`.
