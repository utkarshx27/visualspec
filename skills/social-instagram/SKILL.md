# social-instagram

## Formats & Dimensions
- `feed_portrait`: 1080x1350 (4:5 aspect ratio) — **Preferred format for maximum feed screen real estate**.
- `square`: 1080x1080 (1:1 aspect ratio).
- `story`: 1080x1920 (9:16 aspect ratio).

## Safe Margins
- Feed Portrait: Top/Bottom 72px, Left/Right 64px.
- Story: Top/Bottom 250px (to avoid profile icon and reply bar), Left/Right 70px.

## Design Guidance
- Mobile-first viewing: Use large, bold headlines (`xl` or `2xl`).
- High aesthetic polish, vibrant lighting accents, or sleek dark mode gradients.
- Keep total text blocks to ≤ 3 to avoid feed clutter.
